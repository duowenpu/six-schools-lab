Six Schools Lab · an Omnigent research lab · run replay

Were the schools discovered — or invented?

Every field inherits its categories. Modern AI cannot tell us whether they are real, because it has memorized the labels. This lab audits a taxonomy with two time capsules that the labels never reached, and reports what its own skeptic could not break.

A sealed text

A silk manuscript buried at Mawangdui in 168 BCE, before the "six schools" were first written down and before Han editors compiled the books we read today. Rival taxonomies are trained on the transmitted books and tested on the manuscript.

A sealed reader

A 13B language model trained only on text from before 1931 reads philosophers who were later sorted into "analytic" and "continental". A modern model reads the same pages. The difference is hindsight, measured.

What survived two independent runs

The whole study was run twice, on two machines, with separate research records, the model vendors swapped, and no shared taxonomies. Run A followed a fixed protocol; run B designed its own study. A finding is reported as a result only if both runs agree.

The lab

Nine specialist agents on two model vendors and two harnesses, orchestrated by Omnigent. No agent has file or shell access: each acts only through audited lab tools, and policies in the orchestration layer enforce the experimental controls.

Taxonomy tournament — early Chinese corpus

Each rival taxonomy was formalized by an advocate agent with citations and frozen before any result existed. Score: leave one book out, and ask whether its passages fall nearest to the other books that share its label. Bars show the chance-corrected score against a book-level permutation null.

The decision gate

After the first result the skeptic attacked it, and the planner had to choose between competing follow-up experiments under a fixed credit budget.

Instrument validation and the tomb test

Before any conclusion may be published, the instrument must find chapters that scholars already regard as Daoist-leaning inside "Legalist" books. Then the sealed manuscript gives its verdict.

Can a modern model read blind?

Blind readers saw passages with every name masked and were asked to name the source book.

The sealed reader — philosophy before 1931

The same tournament, run on what a 1930 model sees, what a modern model sees, and plain vocabulary. The hindsight gap compares the modern model's advantage on a label coined later with its advantage on a label that already existed in 1930.
